How a bag is made

From one hide to one bag

There is no factory and no production line. Every step happens at the same bench, in the same hands.

  1. i.

    Choose the hide

    Full hides are chosen by hand for softness and an even grain, then matched to the plant the bag will be paired with.

  2. ii.

    Cut the strips

    The leather is cut into strips, cords or discs with a knife and ruler. A single bag can need seventy metres of strip.

  3. iii.

    Twist and knot

    Strip by strip, the body is twisted, folded and knotted into shape. This is the slow part, and the part no machine can do.

  4. iv.

    Finish by hand

    Handles are rolled and wrapped, edges burnished, and every knot checked before the bag is signed and wrapped.

The maker

One pair of hands

“I want each bag to feel like it grew, not like it was manufactured.”

Changying is a one-woman studio. She designs every bag, cuts every strip and ties every knot herself. That means fewer bags each year, and every one of them has been held, turned and finished by the person who imagined it.

Because each piece is made to order, colours, sizes and handles can be adjusted for you.
